This presentation introduces **Apache Hive**, an essential data warehouse software designed to manage and query extensive datasets within the **Hadoop ecosystem**. The guide outlines how the platform utilizes a **SQL-like language** known as HiveQL, which simplifies complex data operations for those familiar with traditional relational databases. It explains the system's architecture, detailing how user queries are translated into **MapReduce jobs** to process information across distributed clusters. Key components such as the **metastore**, driver, and compiler are highlighted   to show how they collaborate to organize unstructured data into structured tables. Additionally, the source emphasizes Hive's ability to provide **scalability and fault tolerance** while handling massive volumes of information. Finally, the tutorial clarifies the distinctions between Hive and standard databases, focusing on its specialized role in **big data analytics**.
